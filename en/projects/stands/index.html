Stands

The key to designing stands is to attract people in seconds and hold them just long enough for the brand to speak for itself.

Category
Events
Client
Varios
Location
Madrid

At a trade fair, a stand competes with hundreds of stimuli at once. This series brings together projects with the same starting point: each brand's identity.

Rather than applying a style of our own, in each case we bring into the space what the brand already is (its visual language, its tone and the experience it wants to create) so that it is recognised at a glance from the aisle and experienced once inside.

Graphics, materials, lighting and furniture are chosen to match that identity, always with the same aim: stand out in a crowded setting, communicate fast and work well day to day at the fair. The result is very different spaces, united by the same way of working.
