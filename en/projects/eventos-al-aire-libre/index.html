Outdoor events

Working outdoors made me design with light, weather and the flow of people as living variables, not constants.

Category

Proposals that balance brand identity and the outdoor setting, using neutral palettes, soft lighting and details that respect the space

Each proposal approaches brand identity from a naturalistic angle: honest materials, clean geometries and lighting that follows the rhythm of the day.

The space is designed to guide visitors, highlight key areas and make sure the brand's presence feels organic. The combination of light structures, soft textures and ambient light creates a whole that is functional, recognisable and fully integrated into its surroundings.
