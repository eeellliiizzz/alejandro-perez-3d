Sunglass Hut Summer Event

Designing for Interaction, Content and Connection

Category
Event
Client
Equipo Singular
Location
Barcelona

Held in Barcelona this summer, the Sunglass Hut Summer Event brought together influencers and content creators to discover the brand's latest eyewear collections.

Conceived as an immersive brand experience, the event showcased different styles and product categories through a vibrant, social environment designed to encourage discovery, interaction and content creation.
