Patrón Tequila

Spaces where Patrón isn't just seen, it's lived and enjoyed.

Category
Brand visibility
Client
Mälmo BTL · Bacardí
Location
Madrid, Spain

Immersive installations to strengthen Patrón's presence in high-energy nightlife settings

Designer and renderer of a set of brand installations for nightclubs, aimed at raising the perception of Patrón in places where the experience comes first.

The work combined light features, premium materials and architectural details designed to fit in without taking over the venue's identity. The result: high-impact visibility points that multiply the brand's presence without breaking the rhythm of the space.
