Naturgy

Corporate Event Design for Energy & Social Impact

Category
Corporate event
Client
Equipo Singular
Location
Valencia

Held in Valencia in mid-June, this event celebrated the work of organisations and initiatives that made a meaningful contribution to the region in the aftermath of the DANA floods.

One year on from the disaster, the awards recognised outstanding projects in the energy and social impact sectors, highlighting their role in supporting recovery, resilience and local communities.
