Logiplay 2025 and Rioja 100th Anniversary events

Design to stir emotions and fix memories.

Category
Events
Client
Equipo Singular
Location
Madrid, Spain

Two different challenges, one line: turning spaces into memorable experiences

Both projects took place in the screening rooms of Cine Callao, a venue with a character of its own that became the starting point for two completely different atmospheres.

For Logiplay 2025, the challenge was to take the space towards a technological, dynamic language, full of rhythm and visual energy. For Rioja's 100th anniversary, by contrast, the same setting became a warm, sophisticated stage full of identity. Two brands, two worlds and a single space reinterpreted through design to tell radically different stories.
